## Market backdrop (Costa Rica, 2025–26)

- Intl arrivals 2025: 2,943,991 (+0.8%) — weakest growth of the decade; ~93.8%
  of 2019 (DERIVED). 2026 H1: 1,733,596 (~+7.0%, DERIVED) but concentrated at
  Liberia, not the San José gateway that feeds Arenal.
- Colón moved ₡650s → ₡400s since 2022 (20-yr high), compressing dollar-revenue
  margins of colón-cost operators; 22,000+ tourism jobs lost.
- Spend/person/night via SJO ~$122–137 (DERIVED) — the ceiling on mainstream
  mid-market ADR.
- National ADR fell in 2025 for the first time since '23 (CoStar/STR); growth
  concentrated in Guanacaste and Upper-Upscale/Luxury; mid-range flat.

## Land, conservation & incentives

- PSA / FONAFIFO forest protection: ₡105k–185k/ha (5-yr contract). ~$63k total
  over 5 yrs on ~170 ha — narrative, not yield.
- Jurisdictional forest carbon (World Bank FCPF ERPA): ~$5.00/tCO₂ (DERIVED) —
  does not underwrite an acquisition.
- Contrato Turístico (Ley 6990): 7-yr term for lodging ≥10 rooms; duty/tax
  exemption on construction materials & FF&E, up to 6 yrs property-tax relief,
  accelerated depreciation, unrestricted-hours liquor licence.

## Transaction cost & tax (Costa Rica) — verify current

- Total closing costs ~3.5–4.5%; transfer tax 1.5%; stamps ~0.5%; notary/legal
  1–1.5% each.
- Annual property tax 0.25% of registered value; luxury-home (solidario) tax
  above the construction-value threshold.
- Capital gains 15% on net gain (2.25%-of-gross election available once for
  pre-2019 assets). 2019 reform (Ley 9635) caught share sales of real-estate
  companies.
- Labour: Código de Trabajo art. 37 — contracts survive the sale; seller
  solidarily liable only 6 months. Cesantía 19.5–22 days/yr, capped at 8 yrs;
  preaviso + aguinaldo + vacaciones additional. Employer social charges ~27%+ of
  payroll (verify current rate).
